#AI The Past and the Present

## Module Information
- **Module**: Artificial Intelligence (CMPG 313)
- **Assessment**: Practical Lab 3 – AI History and Modern Comparison
- **Objective**: Explore the evolution of AI by implementing a rule-based chatbot (ELIZA) and a modern instruction-based LLM. Compare both systems experimentally and reflect on their differences.

---

## Learning Outcomes
- Implement past AI using a rule-based chatbot (ELIZA).
- Implement modern AI using a Large Language Model (LLM) via Hugging Face or similar.
- Compare both systems experimentally with identical prompts.
- Answer conceptual MCQs to demonstrate understanding of detection, confidence, and limitations.

---

##Project Files
You will receive:
- `eliza.py` (starter script for rule-based chatbot)
- `LLM.py` (starter script for modern AI chatbot)
- `chat_comparison.py` (script for side-by-side comparison)

##  Notes
This lab highlights the **evolution of AI**:
- Early chatbots (ELIZA) relied on simple rule-based systems.
- Modern LLMs leverage deep learning and contextual reasoning.
- Comparing both systems demonstrates the progress from symbolic AI to statistical and neural approaches.
